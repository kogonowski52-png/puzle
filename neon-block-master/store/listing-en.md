# Neon Block Master – Puzzle

Short description (under 80 characters):
Neon blocks, three ways to play, and a fresh daily challenge. Find your flow!

## Full description

Stuck? Hammer clears a chosen 3×3 area, while Shuffle replaces your pieces without clearing the board. Your starter pack is free. One optional rewarded ad gives 1 Hammer and 2 Shuffles; the same pack costs 90 coins earned by playing. Boosters work in Classic and Time Attack. Daily uses the same rules without boosters for everyone.

Find your neon flow. Drag blocks onto an 8 × 8 board, fill rows and columns, then chain your clears into satisfying combos.

Choose your challenge:
• Classic — play at your own pace and chase a new personal best.
• Time Attack — three minutes to score; clear multiple lines to earn extra seconds.
• Daily Challenge — a fresh goal every day and a calendar of your achievements.

Earn Neon Coins through daily challenges, daily visits, and new records. Unlock Aurora and Sunset themes or the Orbit effect. In NEON LAB, unlock PLASMA with three optional rewarded ads or 240 coins earned by playing; progress never expires. Pick the reward you want, with no random draws.

Enjoy original electronic music, glowing effects, and clear placement previews. Toggle sounds and vibrations whenever you want. Play in English or Polish.

Play offline with automatic progress saving on your device. Includes optional rewarded ads and limited ads between games; ads require an internet connection. No in-app purchases.

No account. No game server. Just your board and your next great move.
